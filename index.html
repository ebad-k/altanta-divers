About Atlantic Divers

Three decades of trust in Pakistan's toughest underwater environments.

Atlantic Divers is Pakistan's first ISO certified commercial scuba diving company. We have served major national organisations for over three decades, providing dependable diving services for dams, barrages, powerhouses and other critical infrastructure.

Our work is built on trust, safety and consistent performance. Our team includes highly qualified divers certified by PADI and NAUI, alongside retired Pakistan Navy and Pakistan Army SSG commercial divers. We also employ SONAR technology for underwater work in dams, barrages and powerhouses.

What we do

Specialist underwater services for hydraulic & marine structures

From gate installation to SONAR surveys, our divers handle the complex, high-risk work that keeps Pakistan's water and power infrastructure running.

Stop Log Gates

Divers position and secure stop logs in segmented weir barriers to create reliable seals for dewatering and maintenance during powerhouse outages.

Gates & structures

Radial Gates (Weir)

Installation, maintenance and inspection of the weir radial gates that regulate flow, with divers carrying out underwater adjustments and repairs.

Gates & structures

Bulkhead Gate Installation

Safe placement and seating of bulkhead gates so intakes and conduits can be isolated for inspection, repair and planned maintenance.

Gates & structures

Roller Gates at Intakes

Roller gate installation and servicing at water intakes. Divers align and lubricate gates underwater to prevent costly operational failures.

Gates & structures

Sill Beam Inspection

Detailed visual and non-destructive inspection of the sill beams that support gates in dams and intakes, evaluating corrosion, wear and alignment with photos and repair recommendations.

Survey & recovery

Underwater Cutting

Removal of metal, concrete and obstructions using thermal lances and abrasive cutting tools, carried out under strict safety controls.

Underwater engineering

Underwater Wet Welding

Wet welding with waterproof electrodes to repair gates, pipes and steel structures without draining or shutting down the asset.

Underwater engineering

Air Lifting & Desilting

Compressed-air lifting to clear sediment and debris from intake areas, restoring flow capacity and protecting downstream equipment.

Underwater engineering

SONAR & ROV Surveys

Mapping of submerged structures and identification of defects in dams and barrages using SONAR, ROVs and direct diver observation.

Survey & recovery

Object Retrieval

Divers locate, rig and safely retrieve lost tools, equipment and other items from dams, powerhouses and intake structures.

Survey & recovery

Intake Inspections

Underwater inspection of cooling water intakes and conduits, documenting condition and blockages so plant teams can plan with confidence.

Survey & recovery

Marine & Jetty Works

Jetty reinforcement, underwater cutting and welding on marine structures, and barge anchor recovery for port and waterside operators.

Underwater engineering

How we work

A disciplined process from first call to final report

  1. 01

    Consult & assess

    We review drawings, site conditions and objectives, then visit the site to understand access, flow and hazards.

  2. 02

    Plan & risk-assess

    A method statement and dive plan are prepared under our ISO 45001 safety system, including isolation and emergency procedures.

  3. 03

    Execute underwater

    Qualified divers carry out the work with surface supervision, communications and standby divers at all times.

  4. 04

    Report & recommend

    You receive photo and survey documentation, findings and clear recommendations for follow-up repairs.

Safety first, always

Pakistan's first ISO 45001:2018 certified commercial diving company.

ISO 45001 is the international standard for occupational health and safety management. For our clients it means every dive is planned, controlled and reviewed against a documented, audited system, not left to chance.

  • Documented dive plans and risk assessments for every job
  • Surface supervision and standby diver on every operation
  • Certified divers: PADI, NAUI, ex-Pakistan Navy & Army SSG
  • Lock-out and isolation coordination with plant operators
  • Maintained, inspected diving and cutting equipment
